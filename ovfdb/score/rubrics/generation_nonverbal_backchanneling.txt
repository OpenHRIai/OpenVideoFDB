CATEGORY: (G) Nonverbal Backchanneling. This category uses the unified backchannel rubric (shared with Verbal Backchanneling): visible cue OR short verbal acknowledgement counts as cue production. Valid cue examples: nod, smile, brow-raise, posture shift, gaze settle, or brief verbal "mhm" / "yeah" (<= 2 words). cue_timing distinguishes realtime listener feedback from turn-based late acknowledgment. Wrong-cue cap: full turn-taking statement instead of brief acknowledgement.
