CATEGORY: (G) Emotion Matching; Face Emotion Display. Valid cue examples: affective signal in either channel aligned with user emotional tone (warmth, concern, excitement, etc.). cue_timing captures whether affective mirroring begins during the user-affect window versus late turn-based mirroring. cue_appropriateness captures semantic/emotional fit given a produced cue. Wrong-cue cap: active contradiction (e.g., cheerful response to user distress).
